Data Sparsity Problem

?

Most possible n-gram combinations never appear in training data. Trigrams are far more sparse — this is the core motivation for smoothing.

Markov Chain Theory

Core Concept
Markov Property
P(wₙ | w₁…wₙ₋₁) ≈ P(wₙ | wₙ₋₁)
MLE Bigram
P(w₂|w₁) = count(w₁,w₂) / count(w₁)
Laplace Smoothing
P(w₂|w₁) = (c(w₁,w₂)+k) / (c(w₁)+k·|V|)
Backoff Strategy
Trigram → Bigram → Unigram

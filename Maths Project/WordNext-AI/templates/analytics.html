Effect of Model Order

Analysis
Unigram — ignores word order. Predicts the most common word regardless of context. Lowest accuracy but always has a prediction.
Bigram — uses 1 previous word. Captures local word-pair patterns. Good balance of accuracy and coverage.
Trigram — uses 2 previous words. More context = better accuracy on seen data. BUT very sparse — needs smoothing more than bigrams.

Smoothing Effect

Laplace Add-1
Without smoothing: P=0 for any unseen bigram. Perplexity = ∞ on test text. Model completely fails on new word combinations.
With Laplace smoothing: Every possible word pair gets a small probability. Perplexity is finite. Model gracefully handles unseen contexts.
Limitation: Add-1 can over-smooth with large vocabularies — too much probability mass given to unseen pairs. Advanced methods like Kneser-Ney fix this.
